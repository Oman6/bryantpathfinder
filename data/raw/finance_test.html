Finance Concentration Curriculum Requirements

Course List
Finance Concentration
FIN 310Intermediate Corporate Finance3
FIN 312Investments3
FIN 315Financial Institutions and Markets3
FIN 370Financial Statement Analysis 23
or FIN 371 Archway Financial Statement and Security Analysis
or FIN 380 Financial Modeling
or FIN 465 Innovations in Contemporary Finance
or FIN 466 Data Analysis for Finance
Two Additional Finance Electives 1
Business Core Requirements
ACG 203Principles of Financial Accounting3
ACG 204Principles Managerial Accounting3
BUS 400Business Policy3
FIN 201Financial Management3
ISA 201Introduction to Information Technology and Analytics3
LGLS 211The Legal Environment of Business3
MGT 200Management Principles and Practice3
MGT 201Operations Management3
MKT 201Foundations of Marketing Management3

A minimum of 18 credit hours is required for the concentration.

A minimum of 120 credit hours is required for graduation.  

1

Must include one 400-level elective

2

FIN 370 and FIN 371 are equivalent courses.  Cannot get credit for both.  FIN 371 needs instructor approval
