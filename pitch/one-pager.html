How it works · the architecture decision

Claude · Language & Vision

  • Reads Degree Works screenshots (when used)
  • Writes plain-English schedule rationale
  • Ranks the top three options by fit

Python · Combinatorial Math

  • Pulls live section data from Bryant Banner SSB
  • Solves time-conflict-free combinations deterministically
  • Refiner runs five critic agents over candidates

The split is the load-bearing decision. Asking an LLM to generate a schedule is how you get hallucinated prerequisites and time conflicts. Asking Python to do the combinatorial math is how you get one hundred percent valid output. Each tool stays in its lane.

Data & FERPA

Pathfinder runs against Bryant's public Banner Self-Service catalog feed (no auth, no PII). The default student flow uses a major-template picker — student picks their major, requirements pre-fill from the 2025-26 catalog, no audit upload required. The optional audit-upload path uses Anthropic's Claude API; Anthropic's enterprise tier provides Zero Data Retention and a contractual flow-down for §99.31 sub-processor designation. No data is persisted server-side beyond the active session in the pilot configuration.

What Bryant gets

For students: registration friction drops from hours to minutes. Real-time seat counts, rotation hints, and professor ratings inform every choice.

For advising: fewer "help me figure out my schedule" emails, more time for the conversations that matter — career planning, study abroad, graduate school.

For Bryant: the first published AI-advising deployment at a New England private business school. Co-authored NACADA 2026 conference session is on the table.
