The Problem

Two systems. No bridge.

Degree Works tells you what you still owe.
Banner shows you what's being offered.
Nothing shows you how those two things fit together on a Tuesday morning at 9:30.

So every student spends their registration week building a Frankenstein spreadsheet — and the advisors field hundreds of emails about why FIN 310 won't fit with MGT 201.

The Bet

What if it took ninety seconds instead of four hours?

Upload your Degree Works audit. Pick your preferences. Get three conflict-free schedules — with predicted GPA, professor ratings, workload, and a calendar file you can drop straight into Google Calendar.

The Agents

Four agents. One pipeline.

Agent 01
Professor Match
Pulls RMP quality, difficulty, and review tags for each instructor. Flags would-not-take-again rates above 40%.
Agent 02
Workload
Estimates weekly hours per course from historical grade distributions. Warns on workload spikes.
Agent 03
Negotiator
When constraints collide, finds the smallest tradeoff that makes the problem feasible. "Drop no-Friday and you unlock 4 options."
Agent 04
Multi-Semester
Plans four semesters ahead. Respects prereq order, rotates electives, balances credit load.

Why It Works

Built by a student.
For students.

  • 01Lived the pain. I built this because I failed to register for FIN 210 three times last semester. Every design choice is a pain point I actually felt.
