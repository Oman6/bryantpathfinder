# 01 — Direct Integration with Bryant Systems

**Question:** Beyond the already-integrated unauthenticated catalog endpoint at `reg-prod.bryantec.bryant.edu/StudentRegistrationSsb/`, what other Bryant-side data can a student authorize Pathfinder to read on their behalf, today?

---

## 1. Bryant Banner SSB authenticated endpoints

Once a student is logged in to Banner Self-Service (the same Ellucian StudentRegistrationSsb / StudentSelfService apps Bryant runs at `reg-prod.bryantec.bryant.edu` and `ssb-prod.ec.bryant.edu`), the JSESSIONID cookie unlocks a substantial JSON surface. The `nubanned` reverse-engineering project (Jennings Zhang, GitLab) is the most complete community map: it documents endpoints such as `/StudentRegistrationSsb/ssb/term/getTerms`, `/searchResults/searchResults`, `/classRegistration/classRegistration`, `/registrationHistory/reset`, and the form-POST flow for `addRegistration` and `dropRegistration` — i.e. the same calls Banner's own JS issues when a student registers a CRN. The companion `UnofficialEllucianBannerApi` repo (alec-rabold, GitHub) wraps the unauthenticated *search* subset for 750+ schools, but it deliberately stops at catalog data because everything beyond it requires a student session. With an authenticated session a student can theoretically have Pathfinder read: their schedule (`getRegisteredSections`), their holds (`/StudentSelfService/ssb/registrationStatus`), their account balance (`StudentAccountSsb`), their unofficial transcript (the StudentSelfService transcript endpoint returns HTML, not JSON, so it needs parsing), and time-tickets / registration windows. Sources: [gitlab.com/jennydaman/nubanned](https://gitlab.com/jennydaman/nubanned), [github.com/alec-rabold/UnofficialEllucianBannerApi](https://github.com/alec-rabold/UnofficialEllucianBannerApi).

## 2. Degree Works URL at Bryant

Bryant runs Ellucian Degree Works at `degreeworks.bryant.edu`. The login page is a thin Shibboleth wrapper: hitting the root issues a 302 to `sso.bryant.edu/idp/profile/SAML2/Redirect/SSO` with a SAMLRequest, the standard Shibboleth IdP-initiated dance. After SSO, the dashboard JS calls two well-known Degree Works endpoints that have appeared at every other DW school I could probe: `POST /Dashboard/api/audit` (the modern v5 endpoint, JSON in/out, returns the full block-by-block requirement tree the student sees on screen), and the older `GET /Dashboard/services/getStudentBlocks` plus `ArticulateAuditService` SOAP shim that DW v4 sites still expose for backwards compatibility. The audit JSON is the single richest data source on a student in the entire Bryant stack — it includes outstanding requirements, GPA, completed courses with grades, declared majors/minors, catalog year, and "what-if" scenarios. It is gated only by the Shibboleth session cookie, so the same auth strategy that works for Banner SSB works for DW.

## 3. Bryant SSO mechanics

Bryant's IdP (`sso.bryant.edu`) is a standard InCommon Federation Shibboleth deployment — Bryant University appears in the InCommon metadata aggregate as a Member organization, which means their IdP signs SAML assertions trusted by every other InCommon SP without per-pair configuration. To register Pathfinder as a federated Service Provider, the path is: (a) Bryant becomes the InCommon "site administrator" sponsor for the SP, (b) install `shibboleth-sp` on Pathfinder's host or use a SaaS like SAMLtest/Cirrus, (c) submit SP metadata to InCommon, (d) wait ~2 weeks for federation aggregate refresh, (e) Bryant IT toggles release of `eduPersonPrincipalName` / `eduPersonTargetedID` attributes for our entityID. Cost: InCommon SP membership runs through the institution's existing membership ($0 marginal for Pathfinder), but Bryant IT must approve. Realistic effort for a sophomore with no IT-Services relationship: 4–8 weeks of meetings, a security review, and a data-use agreement. Not feasible for a hackathon. Sources: [InCommon Shibboleth](https://www.incommon.org/software/shibboleth/), [Bryant Authentication Services](https://is.bryant.edu/services/security/identity-and-access-management/authentication-services).

## 4. Auth-on-behalf-of-student via embedded webview

The pragmatic shortcut is: pop a Bryant SSO login inside an in-app webview, let the student authenticate normally, then read the resulting JSESSIONID + Shibboleth `_shibsession_*` cookies and replay them against Banner / DW JSON endpoints from our backend. This is the same pattern Plaid used for years before Open Banking (capture credentials → log in as the user → scrape) and the pattern Coursicle uses today for its Canvas sync ("log in to your school"). Legally it sits in a gray zone. FERPA itself binds the **institution**, not third parties — a student is allowed to share their own records with anyone they want — so a student-authorized read is not a FERPA violation on its face. The risk is contractual: Bryant's acceptable-use policy almost certainly prohibits credential sharing with third parties, and InCommon's federation policy frames cookie-replay as session hijacking. Practical risks: (a) Bryant IT can blackhole our IPs the moment they notice anomalous traffic patterns, (b) MFA via Duo (Bryant requires it) makes the webview flow brittle — Coursicle's own published guidance is "complete the Duo push, then we'll grab the cookie," which works but breaks every time the IdP session lifetime changes, (c) if we ever store the password (we shouldn't) we inherit a credential-breach blast radius. Sources: [Coursicle 2FA workaround](https://www.coursicle.com/blog/how-to-bypass-two-factor-authentication-for-canvas-and-your-college-portal/).

## 5. Advisor-uploads-once pattern

A cleaner legal path: an academic advisor (who already has legitimate Banner/DW access for their advisees) runs an existing Banner report — the standard "Advisee Listing" with degree-audit attachment — exports it as CSV/PDF, and uploads the bundle to Pathfinder once. Each student in the file gets a stable opaque token they can use to claim their pre-loaded audit. This sidesteps every credential-sharing concern and fits cleanly into FERPA §99.31(a)(1)'s "school official with legitimate educational interest" exception, provided Bryant signs a one-page "school official designation" agreement naming Pathfinder as a contractor performing an institutional function (academic advising support) under the institution's direct control over the data. The Department of Education's own studentprivacy.ed.gov FAQ on third-party tools explicitly endorses this framing. Effort: 1 lawyer-day for Bryant general counsel to review the agreement, plus 1 IT-day to confirm Pathfinder meets data-handling requirements (encryption at rest, deletion on request, no resale). Sources: [studentprivacy.ed.gov FERPA third-party tools FAQ](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/faq/i-want-use-online-tool-or-application-part-my-course-however-i-am-worried-it-violation-ferpa).

---

**Bottom line:** For the hackathon and the immediate post-hackathon beta, the screenshot-upload + Vision-parse path stays the primary input. The embedded-webview cookie-replay path is the realistic Plan B for summer 2026 if a handful of students want one-click sync. Anything beyond that requires Bryant's signature on paper.
